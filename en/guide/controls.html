Controls

Phone (landscape)

  • Left thumb = move joystick (anywhere bottom-left), right thumb = [Attack], 6 skill slots and the auto row.
  • Tap a monster/NPC to target/talk. Drag to rotate camera, pinch to zoom.
  • Long-press (0.6s) a skill slot to remove it. Long-press [Field] in the auto row for auto-hunt settings.
  • No jump (like the classics). Hills are climbed via paths.

PC

  • W A S D move · F attack · Tab next target · 1~0 - = skill hotbar (hunting) · Shift+1~= bottom toggle bar
  • Right-drag = camera, wheel = zoom.

Menu icons (top right 3×2)

  • Map — continent → territory → town. Tap a zone and [Go] to path-find
  • Character — stats & gear
  • Bag — items, put in slot, drop
  • Skills — learn (skill points), put in slot
  • Quests — main & side progress, [Go]
  • Healer — call & set mercenary pet
