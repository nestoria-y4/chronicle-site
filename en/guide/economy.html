Money & Trade

The currency is Gold. It drops from monsters, comes from quest rewards, and from selling gear to merchants.

Between players

  • Stalls — Rent a spot in the central town (fee) and list your items with prices. Gold arrives when someone buys.
  • Consignment — Leave items with the consignment broker; they sell while you are away (5% fee).
  • Warehouse — Store items and gold in the town warehouse, shared between your characters.
  • Drop & pick up — Dropped items lie on the ground for anyone to pick up. Party drops are shared by party rules.

Crafting & dismantling (Dwarf bonus)

  • Dismantle D-grade+ gear at the smith for Dragon Scales (crafting material). Dwarves +10%.
  • Learn a recipe, gather materials and craft gear or shots. Success rate per recipe (Dwarves +10%).
