First 10 Minutes

You start at the character select screen. First time? Press [New Character] and pick a race and a branch.

  1. Pick race & branch — Human · Elf · Dark Elf · Orc · Dwarf × fighter/mage × male/female. Each race starts in its own village.
  2. Talk to the guide — Tap the guide NPC (with ! above the head) in the plaza to start the main quest. [Go] on the quest panel auto-walks you there.
  3. First hunt — Tap a monster to target it, press [Attack] to fight. Turn on [Field] in the auto row (bottom right) to hunt automatically.
  4. Potions & return — Two item slots (HP potion · return scroll) sit left of Attack. Turn on [HP] in the auto row to drink automatically.
  5. Lv 15 → central town — After your race area, everyone gathers at Brightstone Castle Town — shared hunting grounds, parties and stalls open there.
  6. Lv 18 first class — Class quest from Lv 18, 1st class at Lv 20. 2nd class at Lv 40.
