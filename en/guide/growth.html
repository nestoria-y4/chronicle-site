Growth

Experience and level tables use classic values (rate ×7). The level cap rises with each Chronicle (now 76).

  • Lv 1–15 race start area (village + 3 zones) → Lv 15 join the central town
  • Lv 18 class quest → Lv 20 1st class (2–3 per race/branch) → Lv 40 2nd class
  • Learn skills with SP. Grade gear has level limits: D 20 · C 40 · B 52 · A 61 · S 76 (penalty if under-leveled)
  • On death you revive in the area town with 65% HP. No XP loss.
