Loot dice · auction

  • When a party (2+) kills a raid boss, items go to a dice board — 30s, roll 1–100 per item, highest wins. Gold is split evenly.
  • Party bidding (auction): enable it in party settings — the winner pays and the gold is split among members (5% fee).

Siege

Clans take territory castles by siege. The ruling clan collects tax from that territory shops. Siege entry = Crown Shards dropped by raid bosses.
